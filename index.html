    How it thinks
    1. Minimax. It plays every move forward and assumes you answer with your best reply.
    2. Alpha-beta pruning. Once a move is proven worse than one it already has, the rest of that branch is skipped.
    3. Iterative deepening. It searches 1 move ahead, then 2, then 3, and each round decides which moves to try first in the next.
    4. Memory. A position reached again through a different move order is looked up in a table instead of searched twice.
    5. Judging a position. When it can't see to the end, it counts open lines of two and three and favours the centre column.
